About Livestock Palace

Livestock Palace is an operating livestock and poultry business based in Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

We supply farm-fresh eggs, live chickens, goats, and rabbits to individuals, families, and other interested buyers.

Our customers can enquire about current prices and availability, arrange farm pickup, or request delivery to selected locations.

Delivery & Farm Pickup

At Livestock Palace, we offer convenient purchasing arrangements for our customers.

Customers can collect their orders directly from our farm located in Teacher Mante, Eastern Region.

Our Delivery Areas

We arrange deliveries to selected locations, including:

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our farm products, orders and delivery.

How do I place an order?

Browse our products and contact Livestock Palace through WhatsApp or phone. Our team will help you confirm prices, quantities and availability.

Where is Livestock Palace located?

Our farm is located in Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery can be arranged to selected areas including Dome Kwabenya, Achimota, Sowutuom, Nsawam, Pokuase and Amasaman.

Yes. Customers can arrange to collect their orders directly from our farm in Teacher Mante.
